Market Frictions

The Off-Campus Housing Crisis

Over 70% of university students in Kenya live off-campus, facing severe security and economic friction.

Middleman Exploitation

Unregulated brokers charge steep viewing fees and deposits with zero guarantee of property authenticity or safety.

Geographic Friction

Students waste days walking around campus zones (Tudor, Buxton, Kisauni) trying to find vacant bedsitters and single rooms.

Landlord Vacancy Losses

Property owners rely on outdated signposts, leading to vacant units and lost rental income during semester starts.
